# Additional assayed lysin source mappings

Four primary-study sources were audited for additional LysB controls. Full research articles report functional evidence for both LysA and LysB in Giles, PDRPxv and BTCU-1. An [FBR-2022 conference abstract (P-77)](https://acbrdu.edu/pdf/Abstract_Book_FBR-2022_25102022.pdf) also reports recombinant LysA and LysB assays for RitSun; its later [full paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-68636-9) characterizes LysB alone. All four source genomes have zero intervening CDS between annotated LysA and LysB.

The deposited source features are Giles ABW88426.1/ABW88427.1, PDRPxv AKF12448.1/AKF12449.1, BTCU-1 AGI61688.1/AGI61689.1 and RitSun QXG07407.1/QXG07408.1. RitSun's published LysB primers map to its deposited gene-38 CDS after removal of restriction tails. Its conference abstract reports LysA assays but does not identify the tested protein accession or construct sequence, so QXG07407.1 remains a source annotation rather than a verified assay allele. The PDRPxv study identifies genome KR029087.1 and LysA/LysB genes 51/52, and the primer-binding ends support the source mapping; however, its deposited LysB CDS is 1,356 nt while the paper reports a 1,355-bp gene, and the recombinant construct sequence is not available to resolve that difference. Giles LysB has a native deletion phenotype and complementation evidence. BTCU-1's forward primer differs from the deposited CDS at one synonymous position; with the reported 741-bp amplicon and matching reverse primer, the implied translated protein is identical to AGI61689.1, although the cloned DNA insert itself was not sequenced in the report.

These data add independently studied, linked source controls and improve the functional evidence map. They do not supply a displaced experimentally supported architecture. Protein-level or gene-level assays remain distinct from evidence that a given phage requires both enzymes during native infection. The original 20-genome cohort, its classifications and the primary neighborhood-conservation comparison remain unchanged and unresolved.
