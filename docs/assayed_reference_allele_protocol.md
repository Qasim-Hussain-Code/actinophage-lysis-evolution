# Mapping additional experimentally assayed lysins

This follow-up examines four primary-literature sources selected because they report an experimentally tested mycobacteriophage LysB: Giles, PDRPxv, BTCU-1 and RitSun. The source accession, protein accession, gene number, deposited translation and assay scope are checked against version-pinned GenBank records. A source joins the mapping table only when the paper identifies the genome/gene and the record provides a compatible feature and translation; reported cloning primers are checked where available. This is a targeted control audit, not a prevalence sample.

For each source, record LysA and LysB coordinates, protein identifiers and lengths, the number of intervening annotated CDS, overlap or nucleotide gap, and the level of direct functional evidence. Distinguish protein assays from native phage genetics. Generic ester or antimycobacterial assays do not establish native mycolate-substrate cleavage or a requirement during phage infection. A/B adjacency in a source genome does not alone establish functional cooperation.

Conference proceedings are retained as preliminary primary reports, separately labeled from full research articles. When an abstract names the phage and enzyme but does not give the assayed protein accession or construct sequence, record the reported activity at the phage level and keep the exact allele mapping unresolved.

BTCU-1's reported 741-bp LysB amplicon is checked against the 741-nt deposited CDS, both primers and the translation implied by the forward primer. Any synonymous primer substitution is retained in the mapping note. The sequence of the cloned insert was not independently reported, so the mapping describes the allele implied by the published primers rather than a deposited construct sequence.

The added sources are analyzed separately from the frozen 20-genome cohort and from the v0.8 control-selection survey. No thresholds, confidence labels, functional assignments, population claims or expansion gates are changed. Recreate the audit with `python scripts/audit_assayed_reference_alleles.py --download`; the four version-pinned GenBank records are stored locally under `.experiments/assayed_reference_followup/`. The raw records remain local; published outputs provide versioned accessions, checksums, coordinates and derived summaries.
