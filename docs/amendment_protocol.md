# Expanded functional evidence amendment

This exploratory amendment was specified on 17 October 2026 after the pilot exposed a functional-identification limitation. The original pilot remains the baseline. The amendment responds to the observed feasibility failure and is not a prospectively independent confirmatory analysis.

The same frozen 20 genomes and original coordinates will be analyzed first. The original identity, coverage and significance cutoffs remain unchanged. A larger experimentally characterized reference panel will be evaluated separately from a panel that also contains published profile-supported predictions. An exact match to a computational prediction will never receive experimental high confidence.

The expanded experimental panel will include only proteins whose assays and source sequence identity can be verified in primary studies. A second evidence tier may use the specific Gordonia proteins evaluated by Pollenz, Bland and Pope (2022), retaining the published HHpred/domain evidence and the absence of biochemical characterization. This tier supports at most moderate computational family assignments. The original experimental-only analysis remains separately available.

Protein assignment will not use the target's neighbors, distance to another lysis protein, architecture category or conservation score. References will be selected by independently published functional families, rather than by whether they produce a desired architecture. Published holin-like predictions will remain low-confidence candidates. Generic esterase or peptidase labels will not be converted into Lysin B or endolysin calls without the stated reference evidence.

Validation will include unrelated structural proteins, nonspecific esterases/hydrolases where a characterized source is available, held-out published proteins and leave-reference-genome-out sensitivity. VanLee and Yvonnetastic will not be used as new functional seeds. Recovery of a published predicted protein validates transfer of a computational assignment, not experimental activity. Functional coverage, conflicts and every changed architecture will be reported before interpreting neighborhood results.

Any newly eligible neighborhood comparison must still satisfy the same homologous-anchor matching, lineage aggregation and minimum-sample rules. Split or multiple Lysin A genes will retain all component distances and will not be forced into a unique pair. Increased functional coverage alone does not establish recurrent rearrangement or conservation above background. Cohort expansion will proceed only if the revised pilot provides a defensible feasibility basis. Otherwise, the remaining limitation and its tested causes will be reported explicitly.

All expanded-panel results are post-pilot exploratory analyses. A `primary` marker retained in an amended pipeline table identifies the inherited numerical configuration, not an independently prespecified confirmatory test. If any post-pilot H2 tests become estimable, their p values will additionally be corrected together across all executed amended panels and cohort sizes; the original table-level corrections will remain available for audit. No favorable panel or sensitivity setting will be selected as the original primary result. Conditional p values still require the stated ancestry and null-symmetry assumptions.

The expanded run will use an isolated, reproducibly prepared analysis directory, reusing the small installed environment and tools. The original outputs, frozen manifests and reference panel will be preserved in Git before modification.
